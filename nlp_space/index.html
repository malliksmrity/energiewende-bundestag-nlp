German text analysis · Bundestag energy debates

Paste German text. See the energy terms and a guess of the party.

Two small models from the Energiewende project run here, in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. The text never leaves your device.

How far to trust it: the term finder is a word list (it only finds words it knows, and cannot tell for from against). The party guess comes from word choice only and is right about half of the time on speeches it has not seen, against one third to one half for always naming the biggest party. It is a teaching demo. Do not use it to label real people.
